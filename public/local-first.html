Your data lives on your computer.

BloomBoard is built local-first. On the Free plan you can use it without an account, and your work is stored on your device first: fast, private, and available offline. Sign in when you want your work backed up and synced across your devices, or when you want to work with a team.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

What "local-first" means: Your tasks and notes are written to your device first, not to a server. Your own work opens instantly and keeps working offline. If you sign in, a copy is also backed up to the cloud so it syncs across your devices.

What Local-First Actually Means

Many productivity apps keep your data only on their servers and stream it to your device. If their servers are down or you are offline, you can't reach your work.

BloomBoard works the other way round. Your tasks and notes are written to your computer first, and the app reads them from your device. When you sign in, a copy is backed up to BloomBoard's cloud so it stays the same on all your devices. Your content belongs to you, not to us.

Where Is Your Data Stored?

The BloomBoard app stores your data on your computer. If you sign in, it is also backed up to BloomBoard's cloud (hosted with Supabase) so it syncs across your devices. Here's what lives where:

Tasks, subtasks & projects Your tasks, priorities, deadlines, and subtasks. Stored on your device, and backed up and synced when you sign in.
Boards & notes Every board, card, and note you create is stored on your computer first. Boards you share with a team are stored on BloomBoard's servers so your teammates can see them.
Goals, KPIs & streaks Your performance metrics, streak history, and badges. Stored on your device, and backed up when you sign in.
Calendar & reminders Events, meetings, and reminders are stored on your device. The Google and Microsoft calendar integrations are optional and keep their sign-in tokens on your device.
Health & hydration Your water tracking and reminders. Stored on your device.
Settings & preferences Theme, avatar, profile name, layout, and app preferences. Stored on your device.
AI usage counters & email cache How many AI requests you've made and cached email previews are stored on your device. When you use an AI feature, the text you submit is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate the answer.

No Account Needed on the Free Plan

On the Free plan, BloomBoard opens straight into your workspace. There's no registration screen and no password to set. Download it, open it, and it's ready.

You only create an account when you want one: to back up and sync your work across devices, to use a paid plan, or to join a team. Payments are handled by Polar (polar.sh), our merchant of record.

Your Own Work Runs Offline

Because your tasks and notes live on your device, your own work keeps running without an internet connection: tasks, boards, notes, streaks, timers, and goals.

Features that need an internet connection:

  • Bloom AI and the AI tools: answers are generated by our AI provider (Anthropic)
  • Signing in, backup, and sync across your devices
  • Team features: shared boards, team chat, and voice and video calls
  • Gmail and Outlook email, and Google and Outlook calendar sync

Everything else works online or off.

What About Team Features?

Team features (shared boards, team chat, presence, and voice and video calls) need real-time sync, so they run through BloomBoard's servers. Shared boards and chat messages are stored there so your teammates can see them. Calls are not recorded.

You can delete your own messages, leave a team, or ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by writing to support@mybloomboard.app. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Your solo tasks and notes are still stored on your device first, even when you use team features.

You Control Your Data

Because BloomBoard stores your data locally first, you stay in control:

  • Delete anytime: delete your account in Settings, then Account, and its synced data goes with it
  • Delete anytime: clearing app data removes the local copy, and we delete your cloud copy when you ask
  • No lock-in: the app doesn't hold your data hostage behind a subscription
  • Your copy stays with you: your local data stays on your computer, even if you stop using BloomBoard
