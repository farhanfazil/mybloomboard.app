Features that use the internet

These only start when you sign in, connect a service, or use a feature that needs them.

Account, backup & syncbloomboard cloud
When you sign in, your tasks, boards, notes and settings are backed up to BloomBoard's cloud (hosted with Supabase) so they stay the same on all your devices.
Team boards, chat & callsbloomboard servers
If you join a team, shared boards and chat messages are stored on BloomBoard's servers so your teammates can see them. Voice and video calls run through our call provider (LiveKit) and are not recorded.
Google & Microsoft integrationsopt-in
If you connect your Google or Microsoft account, the app reads your inbox and calendar and can send emails on your behalf, only when you explicitly trigger it. Sign-in tokens are stored on your device only. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, then Connected Apps.
AI assistant (Bloom AI)opt-in
When you use AI features, your prompt and relevant context (such as tasks and events) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate a response. AI requests are not processed on your device. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
Paymentspolar
Payments are handled by Polar (polar.sh), our merchant of record. Your card details go to Polar and never pass through our systems. We receive your email address and plan status so your plan unlocks when you sign in.
Website live demoanonymous
The live demo on our website records anonymous usage so we can improve it: your country, the site that referred you, and what you try in the demo. It uses a random ID that lasts only for that browser tab. No names, email addresses, IP addresses or cookies are stored with it.
