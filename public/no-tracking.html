What we never do

These hold for every plan, signed in or not.

  • Use advertising trackers, pixels or fingerprinting
  • Put analytics tools like Google Analytics, Mixpanel or PostHog in the app
  • Sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers
  • Use your tasks, notes or messages for advertising or to train AI models
  • Record keystrokes or take screenshots in the background
  • Keep your Google or Microsoft sign-in on our servers
  • Record your calls
  • Back up your work to the cloud before you sign in

Our website and live demo

The app has no analytics, but the live demo on this website keeps simple statistics so we can make it better: your country, the site that sent you, your device type, how long you stayed, which features you opened and what you typed into the demo's search and Bloom assistant.

Each visit gets a random ID that ends when you close the tab. No cookies, names, email addresses or IP addresses are stored with it, and none of it is used for advertising. Please don't type personal details into the demo.

The full details are in our Privacy Policy.
